iPhone & iPad · macOS & Windows · no Xcode

Your iPhone, on your computer.
For you, and for your agent.

DeviceSteer shows the live screen of a real iPhone or iPad and lets you control it with your mouse and keyboard. Your coding agent drives the same device through a CLI, in a session you can watch, take over, hand back or stop.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Free and open source (MIT). iOS / iPadOS 17 or later, over USB or Wi-Fi.

What it does

Live screen, real control

Watch the device in a window and use it with your mouse and keyboard: tap, swipe, type, Home, Lock and volume buttons, screenshots and screen recordings.

A CLI and a skill for agents

devicesteer reads the screen's accessibility tree, taps, swipes, types and launches apps, all as JSON. The bundled skill teaches Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot CLI and others how to use it.

Sessions you can watch

An agent works in a named session. The viewer glows while it drives, and you can Take over, Hand back or Stop at any moment.

Touches with labels

Every tap and swipe an agent makes shows on the screen with a few words saying what it is for, so you can follow along.

USB or Wi-Fi

Plug in a cable, or on iOS 27 pair over Wi-Fi once and leave the cable in the drawer.

No Xcode needed

DeviceSteer sets a new device up itself: Trust, Developer Mode and the developer disk image. Works on macOS and Windows.

Get started

  1. Install the app

    Download DeviceSteer_<version>_universal.dmg from GitHub Releases, open it and drag DeviceSteer to Applications. The app keeps itself up to date.

    Windows builds aren't published yet; on Windows, build from source (see the README) and install Apple Devices or iTunes for USB.

  2. Set up your device

    Connect an iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later and select it in the app. A device that isn't ready shows three steps, in order:

    • Trust this computer. Unlock the device, tap Trust and enter its passcode (over USB).
    • Developer Mode. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, turn it on and tap Restart. After the restart, unlock it, tap Turn On and enter the passcode.
    • Developer tools. DeviceSteer mounts Apple's developer disk image by itself. Nothing to do on the device.

    From the terminal: devicesteer setup --udid <UDID>.

  3. Or pair over Wi-Fi, no cable iOS 27+

    With the device on the same network, click Pair over Wi-Fi… in the app (or run devicesteer setup wifi), then on the device:

    1. Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode. Under Other Devices, tap Pair with "DeviceSteer", then Pair.
    2. Enter the device's own passcode.
    3. Type the 6-digit code shown on your computer.

    From then on the device shows up whenever it's awake on the same network.

  4. Connect your agents

    On first launch the app offers to set up the devicesteer command (~/.local/bin/devicesteer) and the DeviceSteer skill for the coding agents it finds: Claude Code, Codex and others that read ~/.agents/skills, Cursor and GitHub Copilot CLI. Both are links into the app, so they update with it. Change this any time in Settings › Agents (⌘,).

    If it says ~/.local/bin isn't on your PATH, add export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH" to your shell profile. Agents can read the full guide with devicesteer skill.

For agents

Ask your agent to do something on the phone. It starts a session named after the goal, reads the screen, acts with labelled touches and finishes when done. Every command prints JSON; errors say what to do next.

When a step needs a person (a passcode, a sign-in, a payment, a permission prompt), the agent hands the device to you with a reason, and continues after you press Done.

Agents: run devicesteer skill for the guide that matches the installed version, or read llms.txt.

# one goal, one session
devicesteer session start "turn on dark mode"
export DEVICESTEER_SESSION=s4f9a21

devicesteer screenshot -o screen.png
devicesteer ui            # elements with @eN refs
devicesteer tap @e12 --label "open Display settings"

# needs a person? hand off with a reason
devicesteer session handoff --reason "enter the passcode"
devicesteer session resume

devicesteer session finish

Built for personal devices

The phone on your desk has your messages, accounts and money on it. DeviceSteer and its agent guide are written with that in mind.

You're always in charge

Take over or stop an agent at any time from the viewer. Its input is refused until you hand back, and it's told to stop and wait for you.

No approvals on your behalf

The agent never approves permission, consent, sign-in or payment prompts and never enters a passcode. It hands the device to you instead.

One driver at a time

While an agent drives, your touches on the viewer are held back so you don't collide; one session per device keeps other agents off it too.

Requirements

  • Device: iPhone or iPad on iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. Wi-Fi pairing without a cable needs iOS 27 or later.
  • macOS: nothing extra.
  • Windows: Apple Devices or iTunes for USB; a device paired over Wi-Fi needs neither.
  • No Xcode: the developer disk image comes from Xcode's copy when there is one, or is downloaded and checked on your computer. Apple signs it for your device.
