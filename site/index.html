Your iPhone, on your computer.

For you, and for your agent.

DeviceSteer shows the live screen of a real iPhone or iPad and lets you use it with your mouse and keyboard. Your coding agent drives the same device through a CLI, in a session you can watch, take over, hand back or stop.

Free and open source (MIT) · iOS / iPadOS 27 or later · USB or Wi-Fi · no Xcode

One device, two ways to drive it

For you: the viewer

  • The device's live screen in a window, used with your mouse and keyboard: tap, swipe and type.
  • Home, Lock and volume buttons, screenshots and screen recordings.
  • Device settings at hand: appearance, text size, accessibility switches and a simulated location.

For your agent: the CLI and skill

  • devicesteer reads the screen's accessibility tree, taps, swipes, types and launches apps. Every command prints JSON; errors say what to do next.
  • A bundled skill teaches Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, GitHub Copilot CLI and others how to use it.

Built for personal devices

The phone on your desk has your messages, accounts and money on it. DeviceSteer and its agent guide are written with that in mind.

  • The viewer shows who drives

    It glows while an agent drives, and each tap and swipe appears on the screen with its label.

  • You're always in charge

    Take over or stop an agent at any time from the viewer. Its input is refused until you hand back, and it's told to stop and wait for you.

  • No approvals on your behalf

    The agent never approves permission, consent, sign-in or payment prompts and never enters a passcode. It hands the device to you with a reason, and continues after you press Done.

  • One driver at a time

    While an agent drives, your touches on the viewer are held back so you don't collide; one session per device keeps other agents off it too.

Get started

  1. Install the app

    Download DeviceSteer_<version>_universal.dmg from GitHub Releases, open it and drag DeviceSteer to Applications. The app keeps itself up to date.

    Windows builds aren't published yet; on Windows, build from source (see the README) and install Apple Devices or iTunes for USB.

  2. Set up your device

    Connect an iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 27 or later and select it in the app. A device that isn't ready shows three steps, in order:

    • Trust this computer. Unlock the device, tap Trust and enter its passcode (over USB).
    • Developer Mode. Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode, turn it on and tap Restart. After the restart, unlock it, tap Turn On and enter the passcode.
    • Developer tools. DeviceSteer mounts Apple's developer disk image by itself. Nothing to do on the device.

    From the terminal: devicesteer setup --udid <UDID>.

  3. Or pair over Wi-Fi, without a cable

    With the device on the same network, click Pair over Wi-Fi… in the app (or run devicesteer setup wifi), then on the device:

    • Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode. Under Other Devices, tap Pair with "DeviceSteer", then Pair.
    • Enter the device's own passcode.
    • Type the 6-digit code shown on your computer.

    From then on the device shows up whenever it's awake on the same network.

  4. Connect your agents

    On first launch the app offers to set up the devicesteer command (~/.local/bin/devicesteer) and the DeviceSteer skill for the coding agents it finds: Claude Code, Codex and others that read ~/.agents/skills, Cursor and GitHub Copilot CLI. Both are links into the app, so they update with it. Change this any time in Settings › Agents (⌘,).

    If it says ~/.local/bin isn't on your PATH, add export PATH="$HOME/.local/bin:$PATH" to your shell profile.

For agents

Ask it to do something on the phone. It starts a session named after the goal, reads the screen, acts with labelled touches and finishes when done.

Agents: run devicesteer skill for the guide that matches the installed version, or read llms.txt.

# one goal, one session
devicesteer session start "turn on dark mode"
export DEVICESTEER_SESSION=s4f9a21

devicesteer screenshot -o screen.png
devicesteer ui            # elements with @eN refs
devicesteer tap @e12 --label "open Display settings"

# needs a person? hand off with a reason
devicesteer session handoff --reason "enter the passcode"
devicesteer session resume

devicesteer session finish

Requirements

Device
iPhone or iPad on iOS / iPadOS 27 or later, over USB or Wi-Fi.
Computer
macOS needs nothing extra. Windows needs Apple Devices or iTunes for USB; a device paired over Wi-Fi needs neither.
No Xcode
The developer disk image comes from Xcode's copy when there is one, or is downloaded and checked on your computer. Apple signs it for your device.
Languages
The app is in English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Italian. It follows your system's language; Settings › General picks another. The CLI, which agents read, stays in English.
